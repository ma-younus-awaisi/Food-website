Non Veg and Their Health Benefits

Hen

Excellent source of protein. Builds muscles, improves immunity, and supports body growth.

Fish

Rich in omega-3 fatty acids. Good for heart, brain, memory, and vision.

Cow Meat

High in iron and vitamin B12. Boosts energy and supports red blood cell production.

Goat Meat

Low in cholesterol and rich in protein. Supports heart health and strengthens bones.
